Dear Zipline Recruiting Team,

I am applying for the Long Range Platform Embedded Firmware Internship for Summer 2027.
I'm a Computer Science student at RIT in the accelerated combined bachelors and masters
program, with an estimated graduation date of December 2027, and most of
what I do outside of class is embedded firmware. At GE Vernova I built a full I/O subsystem
from scratch in C on an STM32F7 for industrial radios, on the RIT Electric Vehicle Team I
write C++ firmware in ThreadX RTOS for a custom motorcycle VCU with a 300\,Hz control
loop across dual CAN buses, and in my free time I am currently building an autonomous fixed-wing flight
controller on an ESP32S3 with FreeRTOS, PID stabilization, and GPS waypoint following,
on a KiCad PCB I designed myself.

Device drivers, sensor and actuator interfaces, and controls are the parts of embedded
development I enjoy most, and that is exactly what the Long Range Platform team works on. I like understanding how software and hardware work, and fail, together; and I enjoy solving those issues with logic analyzers and oscilloscopes when debugging. 
At GE Vernova, I diagnosed a servo jitter fault blocking a customer demo by ruling out signal integrity and timing before tracing it to a defective hardware component. A few other specifics:

I've written hard real-time firmware in both ThreadX and FreeRTOS, with a 300\,Hz control
deadline on the EVT VCU and concurrent sensor-fusion and autonomous-control tasks on the
flight controller. Before first flight I built SITL simulators to validate PID gains across
32 GPS approach scenarios, 8 headings and 4 approach distances. For protocols and interfaces, I have production firmware experience with CAN/CANopen,
SPI, I2C, UART, ADC, PWM, Modbus TCP/RTU, MQTT over TCP/TLS, and MQTT-SN over UDP.

Zipline is using autonomous aircraft to deliver blood and vaccines to communities that are
hard to reach. That is not a use case most companies are building toward, and it is the
kind of mission I want to be writing firmware for.

Thank you for your consideration.
